Privacy policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Drive Uploader is a private, personal tool used only by its owner. It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses

When its owner signs in with their own Google account, the tool can read and write files in that same account's Google Drive. It uses this access only to upload the owner's files and to check that an upload arrived.

What it collects

Nothing. The tool runs on the owner's own computer. It has no server, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not collect, store or share anyone's data.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared or transferred to anyone. Files go only between the owner's computer and the owner's Google Drive.

Removing access

The owner can remove the tool's access at any time from their Google Account's third-party access settings.
